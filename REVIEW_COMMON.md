# Review common contract

Review the complete change arc, including working-tree changes.  Establish intent and owners
first; then derive 3-6 risks in the vocabulary of this diff.  Findings are hypotheses until an
independent reviewer attempts to falsify them with a fresh read and concrete file:line evidence.

Report only confirmed defects, explicit unproven evidence requests, and TDD verdicts.  Nontrivial
fixes require a fresh cold review round.  A review never substitutes a fixture, executable gate,
or source invariant with confidence language.
